Barangay RHU Services

Ang mga serbisyong pangkalusugan na maaaring makuha sa Rural Health Unit (RHU) ay sumasaklaw sa iba't ibang pangangailangan ng komunidad.

Maternal and Child Health Care

Prenatal at Postnatal Care

Regular check‑ups, iron/folic acid supplementation, at counseling para sa mga buntis.

Birthing / Maternity Services

Normal Spontaneous Delivery (NSD) para sa low‑risk pregnancies at newborn care.

Immunization Services

Routine at catch‑up vaccines para sa mga sanggol at bata: BCG, DPT, Polio, Hep B, MMR.

Under‑five Clinics

Pagsubaybay sa paglaki at nutrisyon ng mga bata, kasama ang Operation Timbang.

Newborn Screening & Hearing Tests

Maagang pagtuklas ng mga congenital conditions.

Reproductive Health and Family Planning

Family Planning Counseling

Counseling sa natural at artificial na pamamaraan.

Contraceptive Distribution

Pagbibigay ng pills, condoms, injectables, at IUD insertion.

Pre‑marriage Counseling

Mandatory counseling para sa mga aplikante ng marriage license.

Laboratory and Diagnostic Services (Basic)

Routine Lab Tests

Complete Blood Count (CBC), urinalysis, fecalysis, at pregnancy tests.

Specialized Screening

Blood typing, sputum smear microscopy (para sa TB), malaria testing, at HIV/STI screening.

Blood Chemistry

Fasting blood sugar (FBS), cholesterol, at creatinine tests.

Communicable / Non‑Communicable Disease Control

National Tuberculosis Control Program (TB‑DOTS)

Identification, diagnosis, at directly observed treatment.

Lifestyle Diseases

Screening at management ng Hypertension (HPN) at Diabetes Mellitus (DM).

Rabies Prevention & Control

Anti‑rabies vaccines (sa ilalim ng rabies control program).

Environmental Health and Sanitation

Inspeksyon ng food establishments, water supply testing, at pag‑iisyu ng sanitary permits.

Dental Health Services

Dental consultation, oral health education, tooth extraction, filling, at gum treatment.

Iba Pang Mahahalagang Serbisyo

Minor Surgical Cases

Circumcision ("Operation Tuli"), suturing ng superficial lacerations, excision ng small cysts.

Pharmacy Services (Medisina para sa Bayan)

Libreng gamot para sa mga karaniwang karamdaman.

Medical Certifications

Pag‑iisyu ng sertipiko para sa employment, school admission, o PWD/senior citizen benefits.

Mental Health Services

Consultations at mental health education.

Medical Outreach Services

Mobile clinics at medical missions sa mga barangay.
